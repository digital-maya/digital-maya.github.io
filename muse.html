Flagship product

Muse is our internal production studio for AI-powered creative work.

It combines structured prompting, generation, asset storage, embedded composition, and operational visibility in one workflow.

What Muse handles

  • image, video, and audio generation
  • tenant-aware workflows and history
  • composition and media assembly
  • durable asset storage and retrieval
  • billing-aware and event-aware operations

Why it matters

Muse is not just a wrapper around model APIs. It is the workflow backbone that lets Digital Maya test ideas quickly, compare outputs, archive results, and turn generation into a controlled delivery process.
