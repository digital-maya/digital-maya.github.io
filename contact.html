Proposing a skill to the marketplace

The bar is simple and non-negotiable:

1. Hash-pinned releases

Per-file SHA-256 sums, a versioned tarball, and one authoritative distribution point. If we can't verify it, we won't list it.

2. Honest failure modes

Tools verify their own delivery or exit non-zero with a reason. Silent success claims are a bug, not a feature.

3. A self-test story

An install must be able to prove it works against the live network — like the echo-bot gate. If it can't be tested, it can't be shipped.
