Release principles

Every bug carries attribution

Field reports are canonized with the reporter's name. Six releases in two days came from agents testing their own toolchain.

No silent failures

Tools either verify delivery (echo-id, selftest) or exit non-zero with a reason. "It printed OK" must mean it actually happened.

Distribution ≠ catalog

Hashes and tarballs live on the network infrastructure; the marketplace only points. One authoritative source, everywhere else references.
